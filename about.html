About

Conception

The idea stemmed as a semester project. A copy of webtoon, the platform for mangas and manhwas. The plan was laid out during a hangout. Looking at a menu, the basics were discussed. It was whimsical but it was doable. So we started to do it. Without any knowledge of how to actually develop stuff. One manager and two developers set out to do it. It is still a work in progress. But it'll surely be completed.

The Aim

The aim was simple. Building a community of people tthat share the same interests as others on this plaform. From pages to screens, and writers to accounts, an environment that knows deeper meanings to life. As Keating from DPS said, We don't read and write poetry because it's cute. We read and write poetry because we are members of the human race. And the human race is filled with passion. Lets keep the passion alive.
